Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your data. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the Service. This includes:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and hashed password when you register
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter manually
  • Usage data — basic logs such as page visits and error reports, used solely for debugging and service improvement

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive personal data beyond what you voluntarily enter as expense records.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used exclusively to provide and improve Spendly. Specifically:

  • To create and manage your account
  • To display your expense history, summaries, and breakdowns
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve app reliability
  • To send transactional emails such as password resets (if applicable)

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to operating the Service.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secured servers. Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms and are never stored in plain text. We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. Upon deletion, your data will be permanently removed from our systems within a reasonable timeframe.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except in the following limited circumstances:

  • When required by law, regulation, or valid legal process
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Spendly or its users
  • With service providers who assist in operating the Service, under strict confidentiality obligations and only to the extent necessary

5. Third-Party Services

Spendly may use third-party services for infrastructure, analytics, or email delivery. These providers are carefully selected and are contractually required to handle your data securely and only for the purposes we specify.

Spendly does not integrate with third-party financial institutions, payment processors, or data brokers. All expense data you enter is input manually by you.

6. Cookies

Spendly uses session cookies to keep you logged in between page visits. These cookies contain no personal information beyond a session identifier and expire when you log out or close your browser. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete it. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the way we handle your data, please get in touch through the contact information provided on our website. We will respond to all enquiries within a reasonable timeframe.
